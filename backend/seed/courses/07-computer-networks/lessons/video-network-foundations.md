# Video: Computer Networking Fundamentals

This video is a comprehensive introduction to computer networking covering how data travels from one device to another across the Internet. It explains the physical layer, network topologies, and the switching techniques (circuit vs. packet switching) that underpin the modern Internet.

Key takeaways include: understanding what nodes, links, and protocols are; how bandwidth, latency, and throughput differ; and why the layered model of networking exists. The video uses clear diagrams to illustrate how packets hop through routers from source to destination.

Timestamps to focus on: 0:00 — what is a network; ~15 min — OSI layers overview; ~35 min — packet switching explained; ~55 min — how routers forward traffic.
