# Video: Agile and Scrum in Practice

This video provides a thorough walkthrough of Agile methodology and the Scrum framework as used by real software teams. It explains the philosophy behind iterative development, walks through Scrum ceremonies (sprint planning, daily standup, sprint review, retrospective), and demonstrates how a cross-functional team moves a backlog item from idea to shipped feature.

**Key takeaways:**
- Why Agile replaced Waterfall for most modern product teams and what the Agile Manifesto values mean in day-to-day work.
- How Scrum roles (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developers) divide responsibility without creating silos.
- The rhythm of a two-week sprint: backlog refinement, sprint planning, daily sync, review, and retro.
- Practical techniques for estimating user stories (story points, planning poker) and managing sprint velocity.
- How Kanban differs from Scrum and when a Kanban board is a better fit than fixed sprints.
