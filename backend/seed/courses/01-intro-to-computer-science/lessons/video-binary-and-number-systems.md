# Video: Binary and Number Systems

This video walks through the binary (base-2) and hexadecimal (base-16) number systems from first principles, explaining exactly why computers use binary and how to convert between number bases by hand.

**Key topics covered:**
- Why transistors make binary the natural choice for digital hardware
- Place-value arithmetic in base-2 and base-16
- Step-by-step binary-to-decimal and decimal-to-binary conversion techniques
- How hexadecimal serves as a compact shorthand for binary patterns
- Bits, nibbles, bytes, and common data-size units (KB, MB, GB)

**Recommended timestamps to focus on:**
- 0:00 – Introduction to number bases and place value
- ~8:00 – Binary conversion worked examples
- ~18:00 – Hexadecimal and its relationship to binary

After watching, work through the Binary-to-Decimal Converter exercise to practice the conversion algorithm by hand.
