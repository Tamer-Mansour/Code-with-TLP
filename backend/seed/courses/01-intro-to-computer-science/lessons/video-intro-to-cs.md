# Video: Introduction to Computer Science

This video from freeCodeCamp.org provides a broad, beginner-friendly tour of computer science fundamentals — covering what a computer is, how hardware and software interact, and the layered abstraction model that makes modern computing possible.

**Key topics covered:**
- The four core functions of every computer: input, processing, storage, and output
- How hardware components (CPU, RAM, storage, I/O devices) work together
- The role of operating systems as the bridge between hardware and software
- Why abstraction is the central idea in all of computing

**Recommended timestamps to focus on:**
- 0:00 – What is computer science and why it matters
- ~10:00 – Hardware components and the memory hierarchy
- ~25:00 – Software layers and the operating system's role

After watching, compare the video's memory hierarchy diagram with the one in the "What Is a Computer?" reading to reinforce the concept.
