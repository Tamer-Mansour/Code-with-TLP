# Video: Transformers, Attention, and Large Language Models

This video explains the Transformer architecture from the seminal "Attention Is All You Need" paper, traces the path from BERT and GPT-2 to modern LLMs, and shows how pre-training plus RLHF produces instruction-following models.

Key takeaways:
- Scaled dot-product attention computes a weighted average of value vectors using query-key similarity scores.
- Multi-head attention lets the model attend to different representation subspaces in parallel.
- Pre-training on next-token prediction gives broad world knowledge; RLHF aligns the model to human preferences.
