# Video: The History of Artificial Intelligence

This video traces AI from Alan Turing's 1950 thought experiment through the expert-systems era, statistical learning revolution, and the deep-learning explosion that led to today's large language models.

Key takeaways:
- Why AI winters happened and what ended each one.
- The pivotal role of data, compute, and algorithms as three independent accelerants.
- How narrow AI differs from the AGI ambitions of the 1950s pioneers.

**Timestamps to watch:** 0:00 Turing & Dartmouth | 12:00 First AI Winter | 24:00 Expert Systems & Second Winter | 38:00 Statistical ML | 52:00 Deep Learning & AlexNet | 1:05:00 Transformers & LLMs.
