# Video: Unsupervised Learning — Clustering and Dimensionality Reduction

This video demonstrates the k-Means algorithm step by step on a 2-D dataset, then shows how PCA compresses high-dimensional data and how t-SNE reveals cluster structure for visualisation.

Key takeaways:
- k-Means iterates assign (each point to nearest centroid) and update (recompute centroid means) until convergence.
- The elbow method picks k by plotting inertia vs. k and finding the "elbow" where adding clusters stops helping.
- PCA finds orthogonal directions of maximum variance; t-SNE preserves local neighbourhoods for 2-D visualisation.
