# Video: Finite Automata — DFAs, NFAs, and the Subset Construction

This video walks through the core concepts of finite automata, building intuition with visual state-diagram examples before formalizing each definition.

**Key takeaways:**
- How to draw and simulate a DFA step-by-step, and why every DFA is a total function on states.
- How NFAs differ — multiple active states, epsilon transitions — and why they accept the same class of languages as DFAs.
- The subset construction (powerset construction): turning an NFA with n states into an equivalent DFA with at most 2ⁿ states, worked through on a concrete example.
- Closure of regular languages under union, concatenation, and Kleene star using NFA constructions.
