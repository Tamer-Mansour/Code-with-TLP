# Video: Introduction to Theory of Computation

This lecture-style video introduces the big picture of the theory of computation course: what problems are we studying, why do the mathematical foundations matter, and how do the three main pillars — automata theory, formal languages, and computability/complexity — fit together.

**Key takeaways:**
- How to think about a computational problem as a language (a set of strings) and a machine as a language recognizer.
- The hierarchy of language classes: regular → context-free → decidable → recognizable, and what separates each tier.
- Why mathematically precise models (DFA, PDA, TM) matter for proving limits of computation, not just for building compilers.

- 10:00 – Alphabets, strings, and language definitions
- 25:00 – The computational hierarchy and where each model sits
- 40:00 – Proof techniques: diagonalization and reduction previewed
