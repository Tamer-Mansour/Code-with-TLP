# Video: Turing Machines and the Church-Turing Thesis

This video introduces Turing machines as the universal model of computation, walks through several tape-trace examples, and explains the Church-Turing thesis and TM variants.

**Key takeaways:**
- TM formal definition: the 7-tuple, the infinite tape, read/write head, and the role of accept/reject halting states vs. looping.
- Step-by-step tape traces for deciding {0ⁿ1ⁿ} and recognizing {ww}: see exactly how the head position, tape contents, and state evolve.
- TM variants — multi-tape, nondeterministic, enumerators — are all equivalent in computing power (though not in time complexity).
- The Church-Turing thesis: why every known reasonable model of computation computes the same decision problems as a TM, and what the thesis does and does not claim.
