# Video: Digital Logic Fundamentals

This video provides a visual, hands-on introduction to the digital logic concepts that form the foundation of all computer hardware. It covers the design and behavior of logic gates, truth tables, and how basic gates combine into useful circuits.

**Key topics covered:**
- AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, and XOR gates with truth tables
- Building combinational circuits (adders, multiplexers) from primitive gates
- Introduction to sequential logic: latches and flip-flops
- How clocked circuits maintain state across cycles

**Takeaway:** After watching, you will be able to trace signals through multi-gate circuits, predict outputs from truth tables, and understand why NAND and NOR are called universal gates.
