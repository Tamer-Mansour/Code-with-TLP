# Video: Binary and Number Representation

This video walks through the complete number representation stack used in modern computers — from raw binary counting to signed integers (two's complement) and IEEE 754 floating-point. It bridges the gap between abstract math and the bit patterns your CPU actually stores.

**Key topics covered:**
- Binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems and their conversions
- Unsigned vs signed integers; why two's complement is the universal choice
- Sign extension and overflow detection
- IEEE 754 single-precision and double-precision formats: sign, exponent, mantissa
- Special values: infinity, NaN, and denormals

**Takeaway:** You will be able to convert any integer or common float to its binary representation by hand, predict overflow conditions, and explain why `0.1 + 0.2 != 0.3` in floating-point arithmetic.
